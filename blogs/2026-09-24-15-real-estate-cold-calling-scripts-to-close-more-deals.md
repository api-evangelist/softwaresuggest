Cold calling remains a direct way for real estate agents to connect with sellers, buyers, investors, and potential referral sources. But a script should guide the conversation, not make it sound rehearsed. The goal is to introduce yourself clearly, understand the prospect’s situation, and agree on a relevant next step.
