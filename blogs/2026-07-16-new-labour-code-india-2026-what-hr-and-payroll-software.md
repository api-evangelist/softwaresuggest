India’s new labour code marks a major shift in labour regulation, bringing 29 central labour laws into 4 simplified codes. As of 2026, rollout is happening in phases through state notifications, with full implementation still in progress. The reforms are expected to impact over 643 million workers across organised and unorganised sectors.
